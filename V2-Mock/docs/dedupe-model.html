WorkIt · De-dupe engine

Can I work this lead? Checked across three systems.

Every lead originates in GMO Salesforce. Before an SDR works it, the engine matches the company against Intacct Salesforce and SAP Fusion to see what the company already owns — and the verdict turns on which product they own, not just whether they're a customer.

Block — don't work it Review — work it, but verify first Pass — clear to work

How the verdict is decided

First find the company across systems, then judge what they own.

1 · Match the company
StrongDomain & website — an exact hit is authoritative.
FuzzyCompany name + address / geography — a name-only match is never a silent block; it downgrades to review.
2 · Judge what they own
Current customer of the exact same productBlock
Customer of any other product same team or notReview
Former customer of any productReview
Fuzzy-only match (name / geo, no domain)Review
No match anywherePass

Which systems each check reads

The customer match always hits both other systems. Opportunity, DQ and activity checks only exist where the deals are actually worked.

CheckGMOIntacct SFFusion
Customer / product ownership origin ✓ always ✓ always
Open opp · DQ opp · activity (ROE) ✓ always Intacct leads — no opps
Partner / VAR ✓ ✓ —
TAM ✓ only — —
Ownership (assigned rep) ✓ only — —

Open opp / DQ / activity are product-agnostic — any open (or DQ'd within the 6-month cooling-off) opportunity signals the account is already being worked, regardless of product line. Only the customer check is product-exact. Fusion products' opportunities are worked in GMO, so there's nothing opp-level to read in Fusion — it's account-ownership only.
